---
title: My Lines Only — running just your part
summary: Strip out every other character's spoken line so a scene is nothing but your own lines.
date: 2026-10-03
---

My Lines Only does what it says: it removes every other character's spoken line from the scene
completely, leaving just your own lines to run. Unlike Cue-to-cue there are no grey "Skip" cards
standing in for what's gone — the other characters' dialogue simply isn't there.

## Turning it on

- In **Practice**, open the ⋮ menu (top right) and tick **My Lines Only**, directly under
  Cue-to-cue.
- It sticks once set, exactly like Cue-to-cue in Practice — leave the scene and come back and it's
  still on, until you untick it. It's remembered like Cue-to-cue and is included in a full
  backup along with your other settings.

## What stays and what goes

- Gone: every spoken line belonging to another character.
- Stays: your own lines, plus stage directions, sound cues, lighting cues and page breaks. Those
  last few fall away only when "Stage Directions" is unticked, exactly as normal.

## Cue-to-cue and My Lines Only

- They're mutually exclusive: ticking My Lines Only unticks Cue-to-cue, and ticking Cue-to-cue
  unticks My Lines Only. Both narrow the scene to your part, but differently — Cue-to-cue keeps
  each cue line feeding into yours; My Lines Only keeps none of them.

## Running with it on

- It works with **Auto run** and **Loop**.
- With Auto run on, a short tap sound plays between your lines so you know the next one is coming,
  then the app listens and scores as normal.

## What gets greyed

While My Lines Only is on, two menu items are greyed out:

- **Shorten long lines** — there are no other-character lines left to shorten.
- **My next line** — every line on screen is already yours.

**Define section** and **Define speech** stay in the menu, but tapping them shows the same warning
Cue-to-cue gives, asking you to turn My Lines Only off first.

## In the Edit screen

The Scene Editor's own filter menu, which used to be called "My character", is now **My Lines
Only**. As before it hides everyone else's spoken lines. Like in Practice, it can't be on at the
same time as the editor's Cue-to-cue filter — ticking one unticks the other.
