Notes are the electronic version of pencil in the margin: a director's note, a bit of blocking, a
reminder of why you pause. They live on your phone, against the line you put them on.

## Adding a note

- In Practice, the leftmost column of the script (headed **Notes**) is the Notes column. Press and
  hold in it, on any line, and a small menu appears just above your finger (below it only for
  lines near the top of the screen): **Add note** and **Add memory**. You can also press and hold
  on the 📝 in the page margin.
- **Add note** opens a note box in the top half of the screen, just big enough for a full note. The
  keyboard comes up straight away below it, so you can type or use your keyboard's microphone to
  dictate. **Cancel** and **Save** are at the top of the box, and the keyboard never covers it.
- A note can be up to 255 characters (an emoji counts as one). The counter at the bottom tells you
  how many you have used; once you reach the limit, typing adds nothing, and pasted or dictated
  text is cut at the limit (you'll see "Shortened to 255 characters").
- **Clear** (after a confirmation) empties the box. Nothing is deleted until you tap **Save**;
  **Cancel** keeps the note as it was.
- If you changed the text and tap **Cancel** or Back, the app asks "Discard your changes?" first.
  Tapping outside the box does nothing, so a half-written note is never lost by accident.
- Saving an empty note removes it.

## Notes on every kind of line

- Your own lines, other characters' lines, stage directions, sound cues and light cues can all
  carry a note.
- A line holds **either a note or a memory, never both**. When a line has one, the menu offers to
  edit it and greys the other, with a one-line reason underneath. Delete the memory, or save the
  note empty, and both choices come back.
- Memories (AI memory links) are only for your own spoken lines; on stage directions, sound and
  light the menu says "Memories are for your spoken lines." See [Memory links](memory-links.md).
- Skip markers, page breaks and lines you have marked to skip can't take a note. A note you put on
  a line before skipping it is kept, hidden, and comes back when you unskip the line.

## Reading a note

- A 📝 in the left margin marks a line with a note (a memory shows its own emoji there).
- Press and hold **anywhere else on the line**: the note appears in a small paper slip just above
  your finger, with a pointer down to the line. It stays while your finger is down, even if your
  finger slides about, and the script doesn't scroll. The line you're holding is highlighted
  while you hold (this doesn't change where your next run starts). Lift your finger and it's gone. Near the top
  of the screen the slip appears below your finger, shifted to one side.
- Holding a line with no note or memory does nothing.

## When it works

Notes, the Notes menu and holding to read only work when the screen is idle or a run is paused —
never mid-run, during the 3-2-1 countdown, or while you're defining a section or speech. A quick
tap in the Notes column is an ordinary line tap (it marks where your next run starts); while a run
is paused it does nothing, so a tap that lifts too early can't restart your run.

## Privacy, backups and sharing

Notes are personal. They are included in your own backups ("Back up this play" and the full .zip)
and come back on restore, but "Export for cast" never includes them. Notes aren't shown in
Rehearse. See [Backing up and sharing a play](backup-and-share.md).

> **Tip:** With TalkBack, a line with a note says "Has a note"; its actions include "Read note",
> "Edit note" and "Edit in Edit screen".
